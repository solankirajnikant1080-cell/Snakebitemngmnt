🚨 Cardiac Arrest (CPR)

  • Check Responsiveness & Pulse (10 sec).
  • CPR: 100-120/min, 5-6 cm depth.
  • Ratio: 30 compressions : 2 breaths.
  • Drugs: Adrenaline 1 mg IV every 3-5 min.
  • Shock: VF/VT -> 120-200 J.

🔴 Anaphylaxis

  • Adrenaline 0.5 mg IM (Mid-thigh) - Immediate.
  • Oxygen + IV NS 500-1000 mL bolus.
  • Monitor BP and HR closely.

🐍 Snake Bite Protocol

Strictly NO Tourniquet / Incision
  • First Aid: Immobilize limb, remove rings.
  • 20WBCT: Check blood clotting every 6 hrs.
  • ASV Dose: 10 vials in 5-10 mL/kg NS over 1 hr.
  • Pre-med: Adrenaline 0.25 mg SC.

💉 High Alert Drugs

  • Adrenaline: 1 mg/mL (1:1000)
  • Noradrenaline: 4 mg/2 mL
  • Insulin: 100 IU/mL
  • KCl: NEVER IV push.
